THE GETAWAY MISSION

Original Story: You are the Underground Syndicate's most sought-after wheelman in Neon Getaway: Destruction Run. Sheriff Vance and the Highway Patrol have locked down the county borders. Smash contraband drops, collect bounties, and outrun the law!

🖥️ Desktop Controls (Mouse-Driven Steering)

  • Mouse Movement: Steer car smoothly towards cursor.
  • Right Mouse Button (Hold): Throttle / Nitro acceleration boost.
  • Automatic Forward Drive: Car cruises forward automatically!
  • Optional Keyboard: W/S for boost/brake, A/D or Arrows to steer, Space to handbrake/drift.
  • Abilities: 1/Q (Titan Tank), 2/E (Oil Slick), 3/R (Electric Shock). Each has an independent 150s cooldown.
  • Auto Reverse: Automatically backs out if stuck against a wall for ~2 seconds.
  • Pause: Press Esc or P.

📱 Mobile Controls (One Circular Touch Ring)

  • Circular Touch Ring (Bottom-Right): Touch and drag within or around the translucent circle to steer.
  • Automatic Drive: Vehicle moves forward automatically without holding an accelerator.
  • Abilities (Bottom-Left): Tap the Tank, Oil Slick, or Electric Shock buttons.

💥 Destruction & Damage

Smashing wooden fences, crates, fuel barrels, road signs, and roadside structures awards cash and builds your COMBO MULTIPLIER. Watch your VEHICLE HEALTH bar—crashing into solid walls at high speed causes heavy damage!
